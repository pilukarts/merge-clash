# Camp environment artwork

Asset: `dist/assets/camp.webp`.
Created with the built-in image-generation tool using the approved Merge Clash poster as a style/world reference; optimized to WebP for the game.

Prompt: premium 2.5D galactic survival camp, slightly elevated three-quarter view; repairable crashed patrol ship in central-right terrain; improvised shelter on the left, workshop pad on the right and a winding exploration trail; unknown alien world, two moons, bioluminescent vegetation and a receding electrical storm; sculpted metal, readable large shapes and hopeful atmosphere; no people, text or UI.

The current environment is one background illustration. Building and repair states are indicated by interactive markers and panels; separate intact/damaged ship and modular repair artwork are planned.


# Story cards and individual hero designs

Built-in image generation produced `dist/assets/squad-v2.webp` and `dist/assets/intro-1.webp` through `intro-3.webp`. Optimized WebP copies are consumed directly by the game.

Hero prompt: five original human MC heroes in sealed practical armor, natural adult proportions, different silhouettes/helmets/poses/equipment: red plasma cadet Fiery, broad green nanobot medic, agile electric-blue female officer, violet female general with tactical display, imposing gold commander with white visor and central crest. Original galactic metal-and-light identity, no recolored clones.

Story prompts: detailed collectible-card illustrations of (1) patrol ship losing control in electrical storm, (2) Fiery waking in the damaged cockpit above an unknown alien landscape, and (3) the commander stopping Fiery from releasing his helmet seal until the atmosphere can be analyzed. Each scene uses its own illustration, while the frame and bilingual story text are rendered in HTML.

The intro is an illustrated three-card prologue, not a video. A prominent camp button and `?intro=1` open it even after it has been marked as seen. Hero images retain their native aspect ratio in the UI. The green medic's combat healing skill remains part of the planned combat update; this change adds the visual design and biography.
