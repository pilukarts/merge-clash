# Merge Clash — agreed design

[English](GAME_DESIGN.md) · [Español](GAME_DESIGN.es.md)

## Identity

MC means Merge Clash. The emblem has a large M with a C nested in its lower interior, inside the M silhouette. Original helmets, sculpted metal, luminous visors and a galactic setting. The medieval arena reference becomes a space fortress; its imagery and characters are not included.

## Arena and strategy

Our squad occupies the lower side, with the enemy above. Troops stand within a scene with depth, shadows, paths and defenses. The player places five soldiers strategically. Matches and the arena belong to the same battle: matches trigger troop actions rather than merely collecting resources for a separate mode. Mobile arena/board placement needs testing. The current panel interface is temporary.

## Squad

| Color | Provisional rank | Proposed role |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Cadet | Fast and inexpensive |
| Green | Soldier | Toughness and protection |
| Blue | Officer | Ranged attacks |
| Violet | General | Support and coordination |
| Gold | Commander | Powerful special ability |

The five fight together against an enemy troop. Color identifies whose energy and action are activated by matches. Range, speed, damage and costs require balance testing.

## Matches

3 tiles: normal; 4: super; 5: epic; 6+: maximum attack, provisionally called supernova. The enemy responds with its own move. Adjacent swaps, horizontal/vertical lines, cascades and turns on a shared board are prototype decisions that may change with testing.

## Missions, ranks and boss

Four rounds per level lead to a final boss. Defeating the boss unlocks the next level and awards evolution points. A final card battle was requested, followed by the clarification that matches move soldiers in the arena. The next implementation must integrate both systems.

Helmets evolve visually after completed missions until commander. The first prototype animates five appearances across the first level. Later visual progression and rewards remain to be designed.

## Stories as tutorials

Each soldier will have a story, personality and motivation. Short playable chapters teach matching, defense, range, coordination and command. Begin with the red cadet’s first mission. Names, biographies and dialogue are not yet defined. Both English and Spanish are required.

## Advanced units

Higher levels introduce cannon soldiers, artillery and tanks, retaining the link between color, energy and strategic action. Their art and rules are pending.

## Collectibles

Validate enjoyment first. The soldiers could later become NFTs if the game attracts interest. No blockchain, supply, price or utilities have been selected. No blockchain integration exists in the prototype.
